Privacy Policy — Gold Rates Pakistan

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Gold Rates Pakistan ("the app") is published by Wraplet. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Short version

There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics. The app itself collects nothing about you. It does show a banner advertisement, and the advertising network that serves it uses your device's advertising ID — you can reset or limit that ID at any time in Android Settings.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or transmit:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, files or any other content on your device
  • any usage, behavioural or analytics data
  • any financial or payment information

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The app saves two things locally, using your device's own storage:

  • Your language preference — English or Urdu.
  • A cache of the most recently loaded rates, so prices appear immediately when you reopen the app and remain readable without an internet connection.

Both stay on your device. Neither is transmitted to the developer or anyone else, and both are permanently erased when you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android Settings.

Network requests

To show current rates the app contacts a server operated by the developer (a Cloudflare Worker), which in turn requests public market prices for gold (XAU) and silver (XAG) from gold-api.com, and the US dollar to Pakistani rupee exchange rate from Exchange Rate API (open.er-api.com). The rupee price shown in the app is the product of the two.

These requests ask only for public market prices. They contain no information about you and no identifier. As with any internet request, the servers involved — Cloudflare, gold-api.com and Exchange Rate API — receive the technical information inherent to a network connection, such as your IP address, and their own policies govern that:

  • Cloudflare — https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • gold-api.com — https://gold-api.com/terms
  • Exchange Rate API — https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms

Note that only the developer's Worker is contacted by your device. Requests to those two providers are made by the Worker, not by your phone, so your IP address is never exposed to them.

The developer stores none of it and keeps no logs that identify you.

The app contacts no other server.

Advertising

The app shows a single banner advertisement below the rates. It is served by Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies).

To select an advertisement and avoid repeating it, Unity LevelPlay receives your device's advertising ID and technical information inherent to a network connection, such as your IP address and device type. It does not receive your name, your contacts, your location or anything you enter in the app — the app has no such data to give.

You control the advertising ID. In Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android you can delete it or opt out of personalised advertising, and the app continues to work normally either way.

Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no other third-party tracker.

Children

The app is a financial information tool intended for a general adult audience. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Accuracy of rates — important

Rates shown in the app are estimates, derived from international spot prices and adjusted by a local premium and dealer spread to approximate a Pakistani Sarafa board. They are not an official rate notification and they are not a quote. Actual prices vary by city and by shop. The app is provided for information only and is not financial advice. Confirm rates with your jeweller before buying or selling. The developer accepts no liability for decisions made on the basis of the figures shown.
